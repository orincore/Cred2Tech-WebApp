## RECITALS

WHEREAS, Cred2Tech operates a technology platform (the "Platform") that enables discovery of government schemes and credit/loan products, and facilitates the onboarding, verification, and submission of applications by Users to financial institutions and government bodies;

WHEREAS, the DSA wishes to be appointed as a non-exclusive Direct Selling Agent of Cred2Tech to onboard Users, verify their details, and facilitate submission of credit and scheme applications through the Platform's Agent Portal; and

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to record the terms and conditions governing this relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement shall have the meanings given to them in Cred2Tech's Terms of Use, which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference. In particular:

(a) **"Agent Portal"** means the interface made available to the DSA for onboarding Users and submitting applications, as described in the Terms of Use.

(b) **"User"** means any individual or business entity onboarded by the DSA through the Agent Portal.

(c) **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including User data, business, financial, and technical information, and the terms of this Agreement itself.

## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 Cred2Tech hereby appoints the DSA, and the DSA hereby accepts appointment, as a non-exclusive Direct Selling Agent for the purpose of onboarding Users and facilitating their credit and government scheme applications through the Platform, subject to the terms of this Agreement and the Terms of Use.

2.2 This appointment does not create any relationship of employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency (in the general legal sense) between the Parties beyond the specific, limited authority granted under this Agreement and the Terms of Use. The DSA acts as an independent contractor at all times.

2.3 This appointment is non-exclusive. Nothing in this Agreement restricts Cred2Tech from appointing other DSAs, or restricts the DSA from acting for other principals, except as expressly agreed in Clause 8 (Non-Solicitation) below.

## 3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

3.1 The DSA shall, through the Agent Portal:

(a) Onboard Users by collecting their PAN, mobile number, email address, GSTIN, and such other information as the Platform requires;

(b) Verify the accuracy, completeness, and authenticity of all information and documentation submitted on behalf of Users;

(c) Obtain and maintain a record of each User's explicit, informed consent prior to collecting, submitting, or processing their personal data, including consent for any credit bureau soft pull;

(d) Compile and submit application packages to the relevant financial institution(s) in accordance with each lender's documentation requirements; and

(e) Perform such other functions as are described for Agents in the Terms of Use, which the DSA acknowledges having read and agreed to separately.

3.2 The specific obligations of the DSA set out in Section 7 ("Agent Obligations and Responsibilities") of the Terms of Use apply with full force to the DSA under this Agreement and are incorporated by reference.

## 4. COMMISSION AND PAYOUTS

4.1 Cred2Tech shall pay the DSA commission/payout in respect of successfully processed applications, calculated and disbursed in accordance with the commission structure, rates, and payout schedule made available to the DSA through the Platform's DSA/wallet dashboard from time to time.

4.2 The applicable commission structure may be revised by Cred2Tech from time to time upon reasonable notice through the Platform. Commission already accrued under a prior structure at the time of revision shall not be affected.

4.3 No commission shall be payable in respect of any application that is later found to have been based on fraudulent, forged, or materially inaccurate information or documentation submitted by or through the DSA.

4.4 All payouts are subject to applicable tax deduction at source (TDS) and other statutory deductions as required under Applicable Law.

## 5. DATA PROTECTION AND CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The DSA shall process User personal data strictly in accordance with Cred2Tech's Privacy Policy and Applicable Law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and shall not use, store, retain, or share such data for any purpose other than facilitating the User's application through the Platform.

5.2 The DSA shall implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the confidentiality and security of User data and other Confidential Information to which it has access.

5.3 The DSA shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without Cred2Tech's prior written consent, except as required by Applicable Law or a competent regulatory or judicial authority, in which case the DSA shall promptly notify Cred2Tech (to the extent legally permitted) before making such disclosure.

5.4 This Clause 5 shall survive termination of this Agreement.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

The DSA represents and warrants that:

(a) It is duly organized and validly existing, and has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement;

(b) It shall comply with all Applicable Law in the conduct of its business as a DSA, including any applicable direct-selling, financial-distribution, KYC, anti-money-laundering, and data-protection regulations;

(c) All information and documentation it submits or causes to be submitted through the Agent Portal shall be true, accurate, complete, and obtained with the informed consent of the relevant User; and

(d) It shall not engage in any fraudulent, deceptive, coercive, or misleading conduct in the course of onboarding Users or representing Cred2Tech's Services.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 This Agreement commences on the Agreement Date stated above and continues until terminated in accordance with this Clause.

7.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

7.3 Cred2Tech may terminate this Agreement, or suspend the DSA's access to the Agent Portal, immediately and without notice, if the DSA breaches any material term of this Agreement or the Terms of Use, engages in fraudulent or unlawful conduct, or if required to do so by Applicable Law or a regulatory authority.

7.4 Upon termination, the DSA shall immediately cease using the Agent Portal and shall, upon request, return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession, except to the extent retention is required by Applicable Law. Commission accrued and payable for applications validly processed prior to termination shall remain payable in accordance with Clause 4.

## 8. NON-SOLICITATION

During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months thereafter, the DSA shall not directly or indirectly solicit or induce any User onboarded through the Platform to obtain, through any channel other than the Platform, any product or service that competes with the Services, using information or relationships obtained through the DSA's engagement under this Agreement.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

The DSA shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Cred2Tech, its affiliates, directors, officers, and employees from and against any claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, or expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising out of or relating to: (a) the DSA's breach of this Agreement or the Terms of Use; (b) any fraudulent, negligent, or unlawful act or omission of the DSA or its personnel; or (c) any third-party claim arising from the DSA's onboarding of, or representations to, a User.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

To the maximum extent permitted by Applicable Law, neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise. Cred2Tech's aggregate liability to the DSA under this Agreement shall not exceed the total commission paid to the DSA in the six (6) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
