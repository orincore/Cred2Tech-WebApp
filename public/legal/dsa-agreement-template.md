## 3. Credit Information Pull — CICRA Compliance and Safeguarding Cred2Tech

3.1 **Cred2Tech as authorized representative.** The Sourcing Partner acknowledges that Cred2Tech has entered into agreements with one or more CICs (including but not limited to Experian Credit Information Company of India Limited) for the purpose of accessing Credit Information on behalf of and as authorized representative of Customers who have provided Individual Consumer Consent. Cred2Tech shall use such Credit Information solely for the purpose of assessing the Customer's loan eligibility across multiple Lending Institutions and facilitating the Customer's loan application with the relevant Lending Institution(s) — whether accessed directly by the Customer or by the Sourcing Partner acting on behalf of and with the documented consent of the Customer. The Sourcing Partner shall not, under any circumstance, represent to any Customer or third party that it has the authority to pull Credit Information independently of the Cred2Tech Platform or its CIC integrations.

3.2 **Mandatory Individual Consumer Consent prior to credit pull.** Prior to initiating or requesting any Credit Information pull through the Platform, the Sourcing Partner warrants and undertakes that it shall:

(a) obtain free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous Individual Consumer Consent from the Customer in the format prescribed by Cred2Tech and the relevant CIC, in compliance with the CICRA, the RBI CIC Directions, and the DPDP Act;

(b) communicate to the Customer the exact purpose for which Credit Information is being accessed;

(c) obtain OTP-based verification from the Customer's registered mobile number as confirmation of consent, in accordance with the authentication procedure approved by the relevant CIC; and

(d) store the Individual Consumer Consent log in a readable format containing: (i) the time and date at which consent was obtained; (ii) a specific "Yes/No" validation of consent; and (iii) the affirmative action record — in any documented means, as an electronic or physical record, verifiable from time to time and permanent in nature.

3.3 **Restrictions on use of Credit Information.** The Sourcing Partner agrees and undertakes that it shall:

(a) use Credit Information solely for the purpose of assessing the Customer's loan eligibility and facilitating the Customer's loan application with the relevant Lending Institution(s), and for no other use or purpose whatsoever. The Sourcing Partner shall cease to use the Credit Information so obtained for each Customer upon: (i) expiry or termination of this Agreement; (ii) completion of the loan application facilitation for which the Credit Information was obtained; or (iii) six (6) months from the date of collection of the Individual Consumer Consent, whichever is earliest;

(b) not retain, maintain, copy, store, reproduce, republish, upload, post, transmit, misuse, use for any commercial gain, or distribute any Credit Information for any purpose other than as set out in Clause 3.3(a) above;

(c) not make any unauthorized use of, or share, any Credit Information Report (whether in part or full) with any other third party, including but not limited to any of the Sourcing Partner's group companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, or associates, in violation of the CICRA and other Applicable Laws;

(d) share Credit Information with its employees or agents strictly on a "need-to-know" basis, while ensuring that all such employees or agents are subjected to obligations of confidentiality;

(e) ensure that Credit Information is duly protected both online and offline against any unauthorized access or use; and

(f) comply with and maintain at all times Cred2Tech's and the relevant CIC's instructions and guidelines relating to data security.

3.4 **Data protection and data purging.** The Sourcing Partner shall:

(a) at all times comply with all obligations relating to Credit Information under Applicable Laws, including in relation to all Sensitive Personal Data and Information received or processed by it;

(b) ensure that all Credit Information shall be hosted, stored, processed, and maintained solely on servers in India and that no such data shall at any time be processed on or transferred to any portable computing device or portable storage medium or exported outside India, including any backups or copies, whether in anonymized form or otherwise;

(c) implement reasonable security practices and procedures, including the International Standard IS/ISO/IEC 27001 or equivalent, and maintain a comprehensive documented information security programme and information security policies in accordance with the IT Act; and

(d) destroy, purge, or erase all Credit Information promptly upon: (i) written request by Cred2Tech; (ii) termination or expiry of this Agreement; and (iii) the date falling six (6) months from the collection of Individual Consumer Consent or the completion of the relevant loan application facilitation, whichever is earlier.

(e) after successfully destroying, purging or erasing all Credit Information the Sourcing Partner shall issue a certificate to Cred2Tech within period of ten (10) days confirming such destruction, purging and erasure.

(f) Cred2Tech shall, upon expiry of the retention period referred to in Clause 3.4(d), delete or anonymise the corresponding Credit Information held on the Cloud Server, and shall confirm such deletion to the Sourcing Partner within ten (10) days of a written request in that regard.

3.5 **Fresh consent obligation.** If the loan application facilitation for which Credit Information was obtained is not completed within six (6) months from the date of collection of Individual Consumer Consent, the Sourcing Partner shall obtain fresh Individual Consumer Consent from the Customer for a further period of up to six (6) months, in accordance with the requirements of the relevant CIC and the RBI CIC Directions.

3.6 **Credit Information ownership.** The Sourcing Partner acknowledges that all Credit Information Reports and scores generated via CIC integrations are the sole and exclusive property of the respective CICs. The credit data analysis deliverables and any derivative output in relation to Credit Information, including all intellectual property rights in relation to their generation, shall always remain the sole and exclusive property of the relevant CIC. Neither the Sourcing Partner nor Cred2Tech acquires any ownership rights over Credit Information Reports or credit data analysis deliverables.

3.7 **Prompt notification.** The Sourcing Partner shall:

(a) promptly notify Cred2Tech if any Customer makes a written request to access his/her Credit Information or any complaint, allegation, or request is made by any person (including any governmental authority) relating to the processing of Credit Information; or

(b) promptly notify Cred2Tech, and in any event within a period of 24 (twenty-four) hours from when the Sourcing Partner becomes aware of any unauthorized access to or use of Credit Information of the Customer. Sourcing Partner shall co-operate and assist with Cred2Tech in any investigation, prosecution, litigation or other action related to the abovementioned event.
